Seniors Program

Bhangra fitness
for every age.

8 free weekly bhangra sessions for seniors 50+. Real folk Bhangra, built for bodies that want to move, not perform. No experience needed.

Why this program

Dance floors left seniors out for too long.

Most fitness spaces are built for someone else's body and someone else's pace. Bhangra was never meant to work that way — it's a village dance, built around community, not competition.

Rangla KC Seniors and KC Bhangra are opening the floor to seniors 50 and up: gentle movement, real folk music, and a room full of people your age who came to move, laugh, and dance together.

Bring your family. Bring a friend. You don't need rhythm — you just need to show up.
